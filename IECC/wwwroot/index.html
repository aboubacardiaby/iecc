WHY YOUR DONATION MATTERS

For years, families in our community have dreamed of having a place where we can pray together, educate our youth, and strengthen our community bonds.

Today, that dream is closer than ever.

We are now in the final stages of constructing and securing a property at 4801 Welcome Ave North, Crystal, MN that will become our Masjid and Islamic Education & Cultural Center.

But this work is not finished.

Before we can open the doors, we must complete the necessary work and final requirements to have this space fully functional.

Your support today helps create a space where:

  • Families will learn the Deen
  • Youth will grow with guidance and mentorship
  • Future generations will experience the Masjid
