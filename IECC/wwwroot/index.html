What We Offer

Programs & Services

From Quran classes to youth programs, IECC Masjid offers a full range of services for the whole community.

🕋

Daily Prayers

Five daily congregational prayers led by our Imam, open to all.

📖

Quran & Islamic School

Weekend classes for children and adults covering Quran, Arabic, and Aqeedah.

🧒

Youth Programs

After-school mentorship, Islamic studies, and social activities for teens.

👩

Sisters' Circle

Weekly halaqas, community events, and support for Muslim women.

🤝

New Muslims Support

Guidance, mentorship, and community for those new to Islam.

🌙

Community Events

Eid celebrations, iftar gatherings, cultural events, and more throughout the year.
