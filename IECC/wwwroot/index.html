Our Mission

A Home for Our Community

For years, families in Crystal, MN have dreamed of a place where we can pray together, educate our youth, and strengthen our community bonds. That dream is now closer than ever.

We are in the final stages of completing our property at 4801 Welcome Ave North as a full Masjid and Islamic Education & Cultural Center — a permanent home for the Muslim community in the Twin Cities area.

Before we can open the doors, we need to complete safety requirements, wudu facilities, and occupancy preparations. Your donation brings us one step closer.

What We Offer

Programs & Services

Once complete, the IECC Masjid will serve as a full-service Islamic center offering:

🕋

Five Daily Prayers

Congregational Salah led by a qualified Imam with a welcoming prayer hall for men and women.

📖

Quran & Arabic Classes

Weekend and weekday Quran memorization and Arabic language classes for children and adults.

🧒

Youth Programs

After-school mentorship, Islamic studies, sports activities, and leadership development.

🤝

Community Events

Eid celebrations, iftar gatherings, marriage ceremonies, and interfaith outreach.

💧

Wudu Facilities

Properly equipped and clean wudu stations for brothers and sisters to perform purification.

📋

Social Services

Community support, new Muslim resources, halal food pantry, and counseling referrals.
