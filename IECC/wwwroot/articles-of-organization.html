Article I Name

The name of this organization shall be Islamic Education and Cultural Center (IECC), hereinafter referred to as the "IECC."

Article II Duration

IECC shall continue in existence perpetually unless dissolved according to law.

Article III Purpose

IECC is organized exclusively for charitable, religious, educational, and community service purposes, including, but not limited to:

  • Promoting the practice and understanding of Islam.
  • Fostering unity among Muslims and other communities.
  • Providing religious education for all age groups.
  • Supporting community service and outreach activities.
  • Managing and maintaining mosque facilities.
  • Engaging in fundraising and financial activities for organizational support.
  • Facilitating interfaith dialogue and cultural understanding.
  • Supporting all activities consistent with Islamic principles and the laws of the jurisdiction.
Article IV Organization & Structure

The Organization shall be governed by a Shura Council, which shall serve as the governing and strategic body.

The Organization shall have an Executive Body (Board of Directors), including elected officers and appointed committee chairs, as defined in the Bylaws.

Article V Membership

Membership shall be open to individuals who support the purposes of the IECC and meet any criteria established by the Shura Council and Bylaws.

Article VI Governance

The governance of the Organization shall be vested in the Shura Council and the Executive Body as specified in the Bylaws.

The Shura Council and Executive shall have the ultimate authority over organizational policies, strategic planning, and oversight.

Article VII Officers & Committees

The officers of IECC shall include, at minimum:

  • Chairperson
  • Vice Chairperson
  • Secretary General
  • Treasurer
  • Social Secretary / Public Relations Officer

The Organization shall also have committees as outlined in the Bylaws, including but not limited to: Youth, Dara, Mosque, Fundraising, Finance, Facility Maintenance and Repair, and Sisters in Action.

Article VIII Meetings

Meetings of the Shura Council, Executive, and Committees shall be held as stipulated in the Bylaws, with provisions for notice, quorum, and voting as specified therein.

Article IX Financial Matters

IECC shall establish a financial system, including bank accounts, financial record-keeping, and audits, as outlined in the Bylaws.

All financial activities shall be conducted in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

Article X Dissolution

In the event of dissolution, after paying or making provision for all liabilities, the remaining assets shall be distributed to a nonprofit organization with similar purposes, as determined by the Shura Council and Executive, in accordance with applicable laws.

Article XI Amendments

These Articles of Organization may be amended by a two-thirds (2/3) majority vote of the Shura Council through the Executive, provided that notice of the proposed amendment is given at least thirty (30) days prior to the meeting.
