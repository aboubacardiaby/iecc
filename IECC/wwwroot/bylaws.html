Article I Organizational Structure

1.1 Shura Council (Consultative Council)

Composition: The Shura Council is the duly elected and organized body that serves as the highest decision-making and consultative authority for the IECC. It consists of the Imam (Chair of the Shura Council) and members of the Executive Committee.

Purpose: To govern the IECC through mutual consultation (shura), guided by Islamic principles and the Constitution and By-laws of the organization.

Philosophy: The Shura embodies accountability to the community and submission to the sovereignty of Allah (SWT). Its actions and policies shall always align with Islamic values, the IECC Constitution, and applicable laws.

Article II Shura Council Responsibilities

The Shura Council shall:

  • Ensure compliance with the Constitution and By-laws.
  • Select, appoint, or remove officers, agents, and employees; define their duties, approve compensation, and ensure accountability.
  • Propose and approve amendments to the By-laws or Articles of Incorporation.
  • Meet at least three (3) times annually to review and approve strategic plans and budgets.
  • Elect members of the Executive Committee and any other committees deemed necessary.
  • Establish election and balloting procedures.
  • Approve any expenditure exceeding $50,000.
  • Adopt policies and plans consistent with Islamic and applicable laws.
  • Appoint and supervise permanent or ad hoc committees as needed.
  • Facilitate fundraising efforts for IECC operations.
  • Appoint three (3) members to the Fiqh Committee.
  • Review reports from all committees and ensure alignment with IECC goals.

Article IV Officers & Duties

4.1 Chairperson

  • Provide general management and leadership for all IECC activities.
  • Implement the Constitution and By-laws through plans and policies.
  • Preside over Shura Council, Executive, and General Body meetings.
  • Serve as the official spokesperson and representative of the IECC.
  • Present annual reports on the Center's status to the community.
  • Maintain relationships with other Islamic and community organizations.
  • Authorize special committees or projects as needed.

4.2 Vice Chairperson

  • Act in place of the Chairperson when absent or upon request.
  • Oversee all functional committees and ensure coordination among them.
  • Manage community affairs and facility operations.
  • Maintain the organization's nonprofit registration and ensure compliance with state and federal regulations.
  • Carry out any other responsibilities delegated by the Chairperson.

4.3 Secretary General

  • Prepare, circulate, and maintain minutes of all Shura, Executive, and General Body meetings.
  • Manage official correspondence and maintain IECC records and archives.
  • Keep updated membership and officer rosters.
  • Ensure all state filings and annual reports are submitted.
  • Oversee IECC's digital communication systems (emails, website, and WhatsApp forums).

4.4 Treasurer

  • Maintain accurate and auditable financial records of the IECC.
  • Present monthly financial reports and annual budgets for approval.
  • Supervise all receipts, deposits, and disbursements.
  • Ensure compliance with financial policies and dual-signature requirements.
  • Serve as Chair of the Finance Committee.
  • Oversee preparation of tax filings and financial audits.

4.5 Social Secretary / Public Relations Officer

  • Plan and coordinate religious, educational, and social programs (e.g., Eid celebrations, picnics, lectures, halaqas).
  • Promote positive public relations and community outreach.
  • Facilitate interfaith engagement with Shura approval.
  • Coordinate social services and community support during significant events (births, deaths, illnesses).
  • Ensure all events comply with Islamic values.
Article V Committees

The IECC shall maintain standing and ad hoc committees to carry out specific functions. Each committee shall consist of at least three (3) members and report to the Executive through its designated chair.

5.1 Standing Committees

a. Youth Committee

  • Engage and mentor youth (ages 12–18).
  • Organize educational, recreational, and spiritual programs.
  • Promote youth leadership and community involvement.
  • Coordinate with the Social and Mosque Committees on joint initiatives.

b. Dara (Education) Committee

  • Manage the weekend school and adult education programs.
  • Supervise the mosque library and Islamic literature distribution.
  • Organize religious workshops, guest lectures, and seminars.

c. Mosque & Facility Maintenance Committee

  • Oversee maintenance, repairs, and cleanliness of all IECC buildings.
  • Manage mosque logistics for prayers, programs, and events.
  • Ensure safety, security, and proper use of IECC facilities.

d. Finance & Fundraising Committee

  • Plan and conduct all fundraising activities.
  • Maintain transparent records of all collections and expenditures.
  • Prepare annual fundraising targets and progress reports.
  • Collaborate with other committees to support budgetary needs.
  • Ensure compliance with all financial procedures and reporting requirements.

e. Sisters in Action Committee

  • Organize programs and educational activities for sisters.
  • Promote engagement, mentorship, and community service among women.
  • Support new Muslim sisters through outreach and learning.
  • Conduct approved fundraising within the sisters' section.
  • Maintain accurate financial records and report to the Finance Committee.

The Sisters' Membership shall elect the following officers:

  • Chairwoman of Sisters' Committee — Liaison to the Executive Committee and representative of sisters' concerns.
  • Sisters' Secretary — Records meetings, maintains communication, and assists in committee coordination.
  • Sisters' Social Activities Coordinator — Plans and manages sisters' programs, halaqas, and social activities.
Article VI General Principles
All IECC activities shall align with the Qur'an and Sunnah.
Decisions shall be made by mutual consultation (shura) and by majority vote when necessary.
No activity shall contradict Islamic teachings, applicable state and federal laws, or the organization's objectives.
Committees must maintain proper records and submit reports quarterly to the Shura Council.
Transparency, accountability, and inclusivity shall guide all actions.
Article VII Objectives of the IECC

The IECC shall aim to:

  • Preserve and strengthen Islamic identity in contemporary society.
  • Foster unity among Muslims regardless of nationality or ethnicity.
  • Organize congregational prayers, Ramadan services, and Eid celebrations.
  • Promote Islamic education for children, youth, and adults.
  • Engage in interfaith dialogue and community outreach.
  • Support family counseling, daycare, and social welfare programs.
  • Encourage publication and distribution of Islamic literature.
  • Develop relationships with other community and faith organizations.
  • Promote understanding and appreciation of diverse cultures and religions.
Article VIII Amendments

Proposed amendments to these By-laws must be submitted in writing to the Executive, discussed, and approved by a two-thirds (2/3) majority vote of the Shura Council.

Article IX Adoption

These By-laws were adopted by the Shura Council of the Islamic Education and Cultural Center (IECC).
