Opportunities

Ways to Serve

📖

Quran & Islamic Teacher

Teach Quran, Arabic, or Islamic studies in our weekend school. Qualified instructors and assistants welcome.

🧒

Youth Mentor

Guide and inspire Muslim teens through mentorship, activities, and Islamic character-building sessions.

📅

Events Coordinator

Help plan, organize, and run community events including Eid, iftars, fundraisers, and social gatherings.

🔧

Facilities & Maintenance

Support ongoing building improvements, cleaning, setup/teardown, and general handyman tasks.

💻

Media & Communications

Manage social media, design flyers, update the website, and help spread the word about IECC Masjid.

🍽️

Hospitality & Food

Help with food preparation and service for community iftars, fundraisers, and social events.

🚗

Transportation

Provide rides to elderly or mobility-limited community members to attend prayers and events.

💰

Fundraising & Outreach

Help spread the word about our building campaign and connect with donors in the Twin Cities community.

🌸

New Muslims Support

Welcome, guide, and support those new to Islam with mentorship, resources, and community connection.
