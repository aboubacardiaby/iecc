Our Mission

A Permanent Home for Our Community

For years, Muslim families in Crystal and the greater Twin Cities area dreamed of a dedicated space to pray together, educate their children in the deen, and strengthen community bonds. That dream became a reality when IECC Masjid was established.

We are now in the final stages of completing our property at 4801 Welcome Ave North, Crystal, MN as a full Masjid and Islamic Education & Cultural Center — a permanent home dedicated to worship, knowledge, and community service.

Our doors are open to all — Muslims and non-Muslims alike — seeking to learn, connect, and contribute to a thriving community rooted in the values of Islam.

Our Journey

How We Got Here

2018
Community Founded
A small group of Muslim families in Crystal, MN began holding informal gatherings to pray and discuss community needs.
2020
IECC Formally Established
The Islamic Education & Cultural Center was officially incorporated as a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization.
2022
Property Acquired
We secured the property at 4801 Welcome Ave North, Crystal, MN — a transformative milestone for the community.
2023
Building Campaign Launched
Community fundraising began to complete renovations, safety systems, and facilities needed for full occupancy.
2026
